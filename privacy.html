Privacy Policy

NTF Backup is used only by its owner.

  • It uses the Google Drive permission drive.file: it can only see and change files that it created itself in the owner's Google Drive.
  • It does not read any other files in Google Drive.
  • It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data with anyone.
  • No analytics, cookies or tracking are used.

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
